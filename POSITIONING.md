# Baller Connection: positioning (v9)

## The one promise
**A coach who sees your reps between sessions.**
Outcome line: *Make practice show up in games.*

The problem we own: nobody who knows what to look for sees a player's reps between sessions. A player can't see their own errors, so the same one repeats. We close that gap with a coach, a short clip and one adjustment. We don't promise results: no real family has used this yet.

## The method
1. The coach chooses one focus from what he saw (an in-person session, or the intake clip).
2. The player sends one clip of 30 seconds or less and one honest sentence.
3. A named coach writes back: what went well, one adjustment, why, next rep.
4. The player says it back in their own words (optional). The coach marks it "got it" or clears it up.
5. The next clip shows whether it changed. Every 4 weeks: week 1 beside now, and the next focus.
Complement: a one-line game note (what carried over, what didn't), and the coach's mark.

## Audience
- **Players about 11–15** who practice alone at least twice a week, first Storm clients who train in person, then remote players who start from an intake clip.
- **Parents** who want one clear next step, one calm note a week, and a private, real coach for their kid. Their questions: *Is a real person paying attention? What do I do this week? How do I stop?*

## Pillars (landing)
1. A coach who looks first (first look within 2 business days).
2. One clip, one adjustment (never an AI).
3. Say it back, then see it change.

## Claims allowed on public pages
Only what `PROMISES.md` lists, each with an engine rule and a test. No percentages, rates, "proven", rankings or results. No AI-coach implication. No film-breakdown.

## What we deliberately don't do
- No full-game film breakdown (30 s practice clips).
- No AI coach: a named person writes every first look, review and reply.
- No streak or points pressure (optional, off by default). No leaderboards.
- No per-clip parent alerts by default. No parent access to a player's private notes.
- No recruiting promises. No in-person or team practice here.
- No open signup: every application is read and replied to by a person.
